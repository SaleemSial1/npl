NPL Teams 2026

Complete Guide to Squads, Captains & Key Players.

The excitement for cricket in Nepal is rising as the second season of the country's top T20 league approaches. Fans are eager to follow their favorite teams as the tournament brings together young and experienced players from across the nation. It also helps local players grow and gives international coaches a chance to share their knowledge. The (NPL) 2026 is expected to bring great matches, surprises, and new heroes for Nepali cricket.

The Nepal Premier League (NPL) is Nepal's main domestic T20 cricket league, organized by the Cricket Association of Nepal. It started in 2024 and quickly became popular because of its exciting matches and strong regional representation. The 2026 season, sponsored by Siddhartha Bank, promises even more entertainment and better production.

Last year, Janakpur Bolts won the first NPL after a thrilling final against Sudurpaschim Royals. This season continues to promote young talent while mixing in experienced players, helping to raise the overall standard of cricket in Nepal.

The 2026 NPL will feature eight city-based teams, increasing the sense of local rivalry and excitement. Matches will take place from November 17 to December 13 at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, Kathmandu. Fans can look forward to thrilling day-night matches under the lights.

NPL Teams

Meet the eight competitive teams battling for glory in the Nepal Premier League 2026. Each team represents a different region of Nepal, bringing local talent and international stars together.

Pokhara Avengers

A formidable team from the beautiful lakeside city, known for their aggressive batting lineup.

Kathmandu Gurkhas

The capital city team with a rich history and a balanced squad of experienced players.

Sudurpaschim Royals

Representing the far-western region with a focus on developing local talent.

Janakpur Bolts

A team with explosive power hitters, representing the cultural heartland of Nepal.

Lumbini Lions

From the birthplace of Buddha, this team plays with spiritual focus and determination.

Chitwan Rhinos

Named after the famous Chitwan National Park, known for their strong bowling attack.

Biratnagar Kings

Industrial hub representatives with a mix of youth and experience in their squad.

Karnali Yaks

From the remote mountainous region, known for their resilience and never-say-die attitude.

Format and Schedule

The NPL 2026 uses a round-robin format where each team plays seven matches. Teams earn points based on wins and net run rates. The top four teams move to the playoffs:

  • Qualifier 1: 1st vs 2nd place
  • Eliminator: 3rd vs 4th place
  • Qualifier 2: Loser of Qualifier 1 vs Winner of Eliminator
  • Final: Winner of Qualifier 1 vs Winner of Qualifier 2

The league begins on November 17, 2026, and the final will be played on December 13, 2026. Key matches include Biratnagar Kings vs Pokhara Avengers on November 18. The playoffs start on December 9.
