NPL Teams 2026

Season 3 franchises, retained cores and auction status

Updated July 6, 2026 - auction live

Nepal Premier League 2026 is moving into Season 3 with the same eight city and province-based franchises. The retained-player window is complete, the July 6 auction is live, and teams are building around their domestic cores before the November tournament window.

The Nepal Premier League (NPL) is Nepal's main domestic T20 cricket league, organized by the Cricket Association of Nepal. It started in 2024 and has quickly become the main franchise platform for Nepali cricket, combining national-team regulars, emerging domestic players and overseas experience.

Lumbini Lions enter Season 3 as defending champions after winning the 2025 title, while Sudurpaschim Royals remain a major contender after another final run. The latest preparation phase has already reported 52 retained players across the eight franchises before the auction pool is completed.

The 2026 NPL is listed to run from October 26-November 21, 2026, with all eight teams expected to play the round-robin league stage before the top four enter the playoffs.

Format and Schedule

The NPL 2026 uses a round-robin format where each team plays seven matches. Teams earn points based on wins and net run rates. The top four teams move to the playoffs:

  • Qualifier 1: 1st vs 2nd place
  • Eliminator: 3rd vs 4th place
  • Qualifier 2: Loser of Qualifier 1 vs Winner of Eliminator
  • Final: Winner of Qualifier 1 vs Winner of Qualifier 2

The current site schedule lists the league window from October 26, 2026 to November 21, 2026. Individual fixtures, venues and timings should be checked on the NPL schedule page as the tournament approaches.
