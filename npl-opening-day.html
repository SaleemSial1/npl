NPL 2026 Begins Today

A New Era for Nepali Cricket

The big moment is finally here—the (NPL) 2026 officially starts today, bringing a month of exciting T20 cricket to fans in Nepal and around the world. With better stadiums, day-night games, new facilities, and top-level cricketers taking part, this season is set to be the biggest cricket event Nepal has ever hosted.

Cricket fans have waited a long time for this day. The tournament mixes Nepal's rising local talent with famous international players, creating strong matchups and memorable moments. Organizers have worked hard to upgrade venues and prepare teams, making this year's event a major achievement for Nepali cricket.

Overview of the Nepal Premier League 2026

The NPL 2026 is the second edition of Nepal's main Twenty20 league, run by the Cricket Association of Nepal (CAN). The event uses a single round-robin format and features 28 league matches, followed by playoffs and the big final on the confirmed final date.
