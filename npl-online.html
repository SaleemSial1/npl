Tips for Smooth Streaming

Get the best viewing experience with these streaming optimization tips.

Internet Connection

Use fast internet (at least 5 Mbps) for HD streaming. Connect via Ethernet for stability if possible.

Device Optimization

Close background apps to free up resources. Keep your streaming app updated to the latest version.

Quality Settings

Lower video quality if experiencing buffering. Auto-quality settings often work best.

Reminders & Notifications

Turn on match reminders in the app. Enable notifications for live match alerts.
