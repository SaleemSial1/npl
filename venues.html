NPL Venues 2026

Tribhuvan University Ground Hosts All 32 Matches.

The Nepal Premier League (NPL) is back for Season 3, bringing top cricket players from Nepal and abroad. This year, all matches are listed at one special venue: the famous Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur. Fans can expect a compact schedule, consistent playing conditions, and memorable cricket moments throughout the season.

NPL 2026 Venues Overview

The Nepal Premier League is one of Nepal's biggest cricket tournaments, run by the Cricket Association of Nepal. It uses the T20 format, which means short, fast-paced matches. Sponsored by Siddhartha Bank, the 2026 season is expected during the November-December 2026 window. Eight teams will play a total of 32 matches — 28 league games and 4 playoff matches. With international and local stars taking part, the league is expected to draw attention from around the world.

How the Tournament Works

Each team plays seven league matches. The top four teams then move to the playoffs. The top two teams play in Qualifier 1, while the 3rd and 4th teams play in the Eliminator. The loser of Qualifier 1 plays the winner of the Eliminator in Qualifier 2. The winner of Qualifier 1 and Qualifier 2 face off in the grand final. This setup keeps the competition fair and exciting.

NPL 2026 Venues: One Central Location

For 2026, all matches will take place at one ground. This makes organizing the event easier and gives fans a consistent match experience.
