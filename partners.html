Our Partners

Official Sponsors & Broadcast Partners

The Nepal Premier League (NPL) works with top organizations that love cricket and want to help it grow in Nepal. Our partners help bring NPL matches to fans through broadcasting, sponsorships, and support. Here are our main partners for the 2026 season.

Star Sports

Official Television Broadcaster

Star Sports is the official TV partner for NPL 2026. They show live matches across Asia and beyond, making sure cricket fans don't miss any exciting moments.

Live Broadcast Asia Coverage HD Quality

DishHome

Digital Rights Partner

DishHome has the digital rights for NPL. They let fans in Nepal and around the world watch matches online, see highlights, and enjoy exclusive content.

Online Streaming Global Access Exclusive Content

Ncell

Powered By Sponsor

Ncell is the 'Powered By' partner for NPL 2026. They support the league with telecom services and promotions to make the fan experience even better.

Telecom Services Fan Promotions Digital Support

X Step Nepal

Apparel Partner

X Step Nepal provides official kits and merchandise for teams and fans, combining style and performance.

Team Kits Fan Merchandise Performance Wear
