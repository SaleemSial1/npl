NPL 2026 Tickets Overview

The NPL returns in 2026 for its second season after a successful start last year. Organized by the Cricket Association of Nepal, this league includes eight teams from different regions. The aim is to promote local players and attract stars from abroad. The matches will be played from November 17 to December 13, 2026, featuring league games followed by playoffs and the grand final.
