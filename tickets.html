NPL 2026 Tickets Overview

The NPL returns in 2026 for Season 3 after two growing editions. Organized by the Cricket Association of Nepal, this league includes eight teams from different regions. The aim is to promote local players and attract stars from abroad. The matches will be expected during the November-December 2026 window, featuring league games followed by playoffs and the grand final.
