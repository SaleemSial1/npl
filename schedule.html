Frequently Asked Questions

What is the confirmed start date for the Nepal Premier League 2026?

Nepal Premier League 2026 Season 3 begins on October 26, 2026. The opening match fixture and scheduled start time will be published following the Cricket Association of Nepal (CAN) match announcement.

When is the final of NPL Season 3?

The confirmed tournament window concludes with the Grand Final on November 21, 2026 at Tribhuvan University Cricket Ground in Kirtipur.

How many matches are scheduled in NPL 2026?

A total of 32 matches are scheduled for Season 3, comprising 28 round-robin league stage fixtures and 4 playoff knockout matches (Qualifier 1, Eliminator, Qualifier 2, and the Final).

Has the match-by-match fixture list been released?

No. The October 26 to November 21, 2026 tournament window is confirmed, while the match-by-match order and daily start times remain pending release from CAN.

Where will Nepal Premier League 2026 matches be played?

Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, Kathmandu is expected to remain central to NPL 2026 Season 3.
