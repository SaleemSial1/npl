NPL 2026 Schedule & Fixtures: Match Dates and Venue Guide

A comprehensive guide to the Nepal Premier League Season 3 match window, venues, participating teams, and broadcasting details.

Season window: October 26–November 21, 2026 · Reviewed 18 September 2026

NPL 2026 Quick Facts (AI Summary)

The Nepal Premier League (NPL) 2026 is set to bring thrilling franchise T20 cricket back to Kathmandu. As cricket fans eagerly await the on-field action, this official hub provides all the verified details regarding the tournament schedule, match locations, and team structures for Season 3.

  • Tournament Dates: October 26, 2026 to November 21, 2026.
  • Official Venue: Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground, Kirtipur.
  • Teams Competing: 8 franchise teams, including defending champions Lumbini Lions.
  • Fixture Status: The tournament window is confirmed. The day-by-day match schedule will be released closer to the opening ceremony.
  • Broadcasting: Matches will stream live on NetTV (OTT) and broadcast on Himalaya Television Network in Nepal.

Nepal Premier League 2026 Tournament Window

The Cricket Association of Nepal has officially set the Nepal Premier League Season 3 window from October 26 to November 21, 2026. This fixed timeframe ensures international player availability and aligns with Nepal's optimal cricketing weather. All eight franchise teams have finalized their core squads through the player retention window and the mega auction, setting the stage for a highly competitive month of T20 cricket.

While the overall dates are locked, the specific match-by-match schedule—including opening fixtures, double-headers, and playoff pairings—is pending release. Fans planning to travel should note the overall window before booking match-specific logistics.

Official Venue: Kirtipur Match Days

All matches for the NPL 2026 season will take place at the historic Tribhuvan University (TU) International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, Kathmandu. Known for its passionate crowds and spin-friendly afternoon conditions, the TU ground serves as the central hub for the tournament.

The ground's pitch conditions historically favor teams with strong spin departments, a factor that heavily influenced the recent franchise auction strategies. As the single-venue format continues for Season 3, pitch preservation and rotation will play a crucial role during the later stages of the league.

Participating Franchise Teams

The 2026 season features eight franchise teams battling for the championship title. The competition includes:

  • Lumbini Lions (Defending Champions)
  • Sudurpaschim Royals (Two-time finalists)
  • Janakpur Bolts (Season 1 Champions)
  • Kathmandu Gurkhas
  • Pokhara Avengers
  • Chitwan Rhinos
  • Biratnagar Kings
  • Karnali Yaks

Each team plays in a round-robin format, meaning every franchise will face the other seven teams during the league stage. The top four teams on the points table will advance to the playoff week.

Broadcasting, Streaming, and Match Tickets

For fans unable to attend the matches in Kirtipur, the league has secured comprehensive domestic and international broadcasting partnerships:

  • Television Broadcast: Himalaya Television Network holds the linear television rights within Nepal, ensuring nationwide coverage of all matches.
  • Live Streaming (OTT): NetTV has secured the worldwide digital streaming rights, allowing international fans to follow the NPL action live online.
  • Match Tickets: Official ticketing is managed through eSewa. Ticket sales typically open a few weeks before the October 26 start date.

Fans are encouraged to monitor the official ticketing platforms for pricing tiers and early-bird access once the detailed match schedule is published.
