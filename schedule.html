Nepal Premier League Schedule 2026

Full Fixtures, Match Dates & Venues

Nepal Premier League (NPL) 2026 is set to begin as Season 3. The tournament window runs from November 17 to December 13, 2026, with matches listed at Tribhuvan University Cricket Ground in Kirtipur. A total of 8 teams will compete in 32 matches, including league games and playoffs.

After the first two editions, NPL returns with a bigger Season 3 schedule. The tournament features some of Nepal's best cricketers alongside international names, giving every franchise a full league-stage run before the playoffs.

Here on this page, you'll find the complete NPL 2026 schedule, including fixtures, match dates, teams, timing, and venue details — everything you need in one place.

Participating Teams and Captains

There are eight exciting teams in NPL 2026, each led by talented captains and filled with star players.

  • Biratnagar Kings – Captain: Sandeep Lamichhane
  • Chitwan Rhinos – Captain: Kushal Malla
  • Janakpur Bolts – Captain: Anil Kumar Sah
  • Karnali Yaks – Captain: Sompal Kami
  • Kathmandu Gorkhas – Captain: Karan KC
  • Lumbini Lions – Captain: Rohit Paudel
  • Pokhara Avengers – Captain: Kushal Bhurtel
  • Sudurpaschim Royals – Captain: Dipendra Singh Airee

International stars and domestic standouts across all eight franchises will battle in Kirtipur, making Season 3 the most competitive edition in Nepal Premier League history.

Tournament Format and Structure

The NPL 2026 uses a single round-robin format. All eight teams will play against each other once in the group stage — a total of 28 league matches. The top four teams on the points table advance to the playoff stage.

The four-match playoff system includes:

  • Qualifier 1: 1st vs 2nd place team (Winner advances directly to Final)
  • Eliminator: 3rd vs 4th place team (Loser is eliminated)
  • Qualifier 2: Loser of Qualifier 1 vs Winner of Eliminator
  • Final: Winner of Qualifier 1 vs Winner of Qualifier 2

All 32 matches will follow official T20 rules under day and night floodlight conditions at Kirtipur.
