Nepal Premier League · Season 3

NPL 2026 Schedule & Fixtures

October 26 – November 21, 2026 · Tribhuvan University Ground, Kirtipur

The Nepal Premier League returns for its third season with eight franchise teams competing across a 27-day tournament window at Kirtipur. Season 3 builds on the rapid growth of Nepalese franchise T20 cricket, bringing together retained domestic stars, fresh auction acquisitions, and high-profile overseas signings under a single-venue format that maximizes fan accessibility and broadcast reach.

The Cricket Association of Nepal confirmed the October 26 to November 21 window, aligning the tournament with Nepal's post-monsoon cricket season when dry, warm conditions produce ideal playing surfaces. All league-stage, playoff, and championship matches will take place at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground, the spiritual home of Nepalese cricket. The complete match-by-match fixture list is expected closer to the opening ceremony.

Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground, Kirtipur

Every match of NPL 2026 takes place at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, situated within the Kathmandu Valley at approximately 1,400 metres above sea level. The ground is the premier cricket facility in Nepal and has hosted all previous NPL seasons, international ODIs, and T20I fixtures.

Kirtipur's pitch characteristics historically reward spin bowling, with afternoon sessions producing sharp turn as the surface dries under autumn sunshine. Teams with deep spin reserves—a trend visible across the Season 3 auction—hold a tactical edge during middle-overs phases. The single-venue format means that pitch wear becomes a significant variable as the tournament progresses into its knockout stage during mid-November.

Seating capacity at the TU Ground accommodates several thousand spectators, with the Kirtipur hillside providing natural amphitheatre sightlines. Match-day fans can access the ground from multiple bus routes connecting central Kathmandu to the university campus.

Tournament Format and Playoff Structure

NPL 2026 follows a round-robin league stage followed by a knockout playoff phase. During the league stage, each of the eight franchises plays every other team at least once, accumulating points based on wins, losses, no-results, and net run rate tiebreakers.

The top four teams from the league-stage standings advance to the playoff week. The playoff structure uses a modified format where the top-two teams receive an additional qualifying path through qualifier matches, while teams finishing third and fourth enter an eliminator. The final decides the NPL 2026 championship, awarding the trophy to the victorious franchise on or around November 21.

Frequently Asked Questions About NPL 2026 Schedule

When does NPL 2026 start and end?

Nepal Premier League Season 3 runs from October 26 to November 21, 2026. The tournament spans 27 days with the championship final closing the schedule.

Where will NPL 2026 matches be played?

All matches take place at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, Kathmandu. The single-venue format continues from previous seasons.
