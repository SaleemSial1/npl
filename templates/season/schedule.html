NPL 2026 Schedule & Fixtures

Broadcasting, Live Streaming, and Match Tickets

The September 7 rights announcements identify separate providers for Nepal television, worldwide OTT streaming and ticketing. Consumer prices, channel listings and ticket-sale openings require separate confirmation.

📺 Television Broadcast

Himalaya Television Network holds Nepal linear television rights. Confirm the channel and match listing with your TV provider; the rights announcement does not specify studio programming or a consumer package.

📱 Live Streaming (OTT)

NetTV holds worldwide OTT rights. Check the provider’s Season 3 listing for country access, devices and pass prices before subscribing.

🎟️ Match Tickets

eSewa holds ticketing rights. The reviewed rights announcement does not give an opening date for sales, ticket prices or seat categories. Follow the provider’s event notice before paying.

Competition rules and the final

The tournament window alone does not settle the match order or competition regulations. Use the published Season 3 rules for points awards, tie-breakers, qualification and any reserve-day arrangements.

When does NPL 2026 start and end?
