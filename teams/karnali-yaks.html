Karnali Yaks

NPL 2026 Team Profile, Squad, and Match Schedule.

The Karnali Yaks represent the strong spirit of western Nepal, showing hard work and growth inspired by the mountains of the region. As the NPL enters Season 3 in 2026, the Yaks aim to perform better among the eight teams. Their journey also shows how cricket is growing in Nepal with both local and international players.

The Karnali Yaks joined the NPL in its first season. They represent Karnali Province, known for its tough landscape and rich culture. Their mascot, the yak, stands for strength. The team gives chances to players from less-represented areas. The 2026 NPL begins on November 17 with 32 matches and ends on December 13.

Team History and Background

The Karnali Yaks were formed before the first NPL season in 2024. Their first season focused on teamwork. Young players gained experience, and the team supported cricket development in remote areas. In 2026, they kept key players and added new ones to strengthen their squad.

Coaching Staff

Former Nepal international Gyanendra Malla is the head coach. He focuses on improving skills and team planning. The staff also includes batting, bowling, and fitness coaches who work together to prepare the team for the challenges of the NPL 2026 season.

Recent Team Updates

The Yaks have retained seven players and added six more through the July 6 auction. This page now tracks only the confirmed retained and auction-bought Season 3 names.

Upcoming Strategies

The team plans to use spin bowlers on slow pitches and rotate players strategically throughout the tournament. They are also working hard on fielding to avoid mistakes and create pressure on opposition batsmen.
