Pokhara Avengers

NPL 2026 Team Profile, Squad, and Match Schedule.

The Pokhara Avengers represent one of the most exciting franchises in the (NPL). Based in the picturesque city of Pokhara in Gandaki Province, the team has quickly built a passionate fanbase and is poised for a breakthrough season in 2026.

After a learning season in 2024 where they secured two victories, the Avengers have strategically rebuilt their squad with a mix of experienced international players and promising local talent, creating a balanced team capable of competing for the championship.

Coaching Staff

The Pokhara Avengers are led by a world-class coaching team focused on developing both individual skills and team cohesion.

Head Coach: Jonty Rhodes

Legendary South African fielder Jonty Rhodes brings extensive international experience and a focus on fielding excellence. His innovative coaching methods and understanding of the T20 format make him an ideal leader for the Avengers.

Assistant Coach: Rijan Prajoo

Rijan Prajoo works closely with local players, focusing on skill development and helping them adapt to the demands of professional T20 cricket. His understanding of Nepali cricket culture is invaluable to the team.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the team's history in NPL?

The Pokhara Avengers made their NPL debut in the 2024 season. While they secured only two victories in their inaugural season, they showed promising performances and have strategically rebuilt for a stronger showing in 2026.

Who is the captain for the 2026 season?

Kushal Bhurtel will continue as captain for the 2026 season. His leadership and batting at the top of the order are crucial to the team's strategy.

Are overseas players confirmed for Season 3?

This page currently shows retained players and auction-bought players from the domestic Season 3 process. Overseas additions will be added only after confirmation.

How can fans support the team?

Fans can support the Pokhara Avengers by attending matches, purchasing official merchandise, engaging with the team on social media, and participating in fan events organized throughout the season.
