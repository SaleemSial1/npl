Sudurpaschim Royals

NPL 2026 Team Profile, Squad, and Match Schedule.

The Sudurpaschim Royals were formed in 2024 for the first Nepal Premier League season. They represent the western region of Nepal and aim to promote local cricket talent while bringing in international players. In their first season, the team improved quickly through smart signings and coaching changes.

The team is owned by Swatantra Pratap Adhikari through Empire Sports. They invest in player health, training, and infrastructure with main partners including Dabur Nepal and National Path Lab. The Royals finished as runners-up in the 2024 NPL, showing strong performances throughout the season.

History of Sudurpaschim Royals

The Sudurpaschim Royals were formed in 2024 for the first Nepal Premier League. They represent the western region of Nepal and aim to promote local cricket talent and bring in international players.

Early Challenges

In their first season, the team struggled with coordination but improved quickly. Smart signings and coaching changes helped them perform better and reach the finals in their debut season.

Ownership and Management

The team is owned by Swatantra Pratap Adhikari through Empire Sports. They invest in player health, training, and infrastructure to build a competitive team for the NPL.

Main partners include Dabur Nepal and National Path Lab, who provide crucial support for the team's operations and player development programs.

Coaching Staff

Jagat Tamata is the head coach, supported by assistant coaches who help with special training and player development. The coaching staff focuses on building a cohesive unit that can compete at the highest level.

Young Talents

Young players like Ishan Pandey and Hemant Dhami show great promise for the future. The Royals are committed to developing local talent from Sudurpaschim Province while blending them with experienced international players.

Performance in Previous Seasons

The team finished as runners-up in the 2024 NPL. They had big wins and strong performances throughout the season, showing significant improvement from their early struggles to become a formidable competitor in the league.

Recent Updates

The team is preparing for the new NPL season starting on November 17, 2026. New partners and new players have joined the team, strengthening their squad for the upcoming challenges.

Upcoming Fixtures

The Royals will face teams like Janakpur Bolts and Karnali Yaks at venues such as Kirtipur. The complete schedule for the 2026 NPL season will be announced soon.

Fan Support

The team has strong fan support from Sudurpaschim Province and across Nepal. Their journey to the finals in their debut season has built a loyal fanbase that continues to grow.
