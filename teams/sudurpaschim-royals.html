History of Sudurpaschim Royals

The Sudurpaschim Royals were formed in 2024 for the first Nepal Premier League. They represent the western region of Nepal and aim to promote local cricket talent and bring in international players.

Early Challenges

In their first season, the team struggled with coordination but improved quickly. Smart signings and coaching changes helped them perform better and reach the finals in their debut season.

Ownership and Management

The team is owned by Swatantra Pratap Adhikari through Empire Sports. They invest in player health, training, and infrastructure to build a competitive team for the NPL.

Main partners include Dabur Nepal and National Path Lab, who provide crucial support for the team's operations and player development programs.

Coaching Staff

Jagat Tamata is the head coach, supported by assistant coaches who help with special training and player development. The coaching staff focuses on building a cohesive unit that can compete at the highest level.
