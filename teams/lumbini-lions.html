Lumbini Lions

NPL 2026 Team Profile, Squad, and Match Schedule

The Lumbini Lions are an exciting cricket team from Lumbini Province in Nepal. They play in the Nepal Premier League (NPL) and are known for their energetic style and strong fan support. They mix young Nepali talent with experienced international players.

The Lumbini Lions were founded in 2024 as one of eight teams in the first NPL season. The team is based in Siddharthanagar, close to the birthplace of Lord Buddha. This gives the team a strong cultural identity. After a challenging first season, they have made strategic improvements for 2026.

Coaching Staff

The Lumbini Lions are led by an experienced coaching team focused on developing both individual skills and team cohesion.

Former Indian fast bowler Tinu Yohannan brings extensive coaching experience and a focus on modern T20 strategies. His innovative coaching methods and understanding of the game make him an ideal leader for the Lions.

Team Mentor: Robin Singh

Former Indian all-rounder Robin Singh works closely with players, focusing on skill development and helping them adapt to the demands of professional T20 cricket. His international experience is invaluable to the team.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the team's history in NPL?

The Lumbini Lions made their NPL debut in the 2024 season. They had a challenging first season with only one victory but showed promising performances and have strategically rebuilt for a stronger showing in 2026.

Who is the captain for the 2026 season?

Rohit Paudel will lead the team as captain for the 2026 season. His leadership and all-round capabilities are crucial to the team's strategy.

Which international players are in the squad?

The Lions have signed several international stars including D'Arcy Short (Australia), Gulbadin Naib (Afghanistan), Ben Cutting (Australia), and Thomas Draca (Australia).

How can fans support the team?

Fans can support the Lumbini Lions by attending matches, purchasing official merchandise, engaging with the team on social media, and participating in fan events organized throughout the season.
