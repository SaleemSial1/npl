Biratnagar Kings

NPL 2026 Team Profile, Squad, and Match Schedule

The Biratnagar Kings are one of the most popular teams in the Nepal Premier League. They are known for their mix of strong international players and talented Nepali cricketers. The team keeps improving each season, bringing excitement to fans across Nepal.

The team represents the city of Biratnagar in eastern Nepal. They joined the first season of the Nepal Premier League in 2024. Their home ground is Baijanathpur Cricket Ground, but many matches are played at Tribhuvan University Ground in Kathmandu.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the team's history in NPL?

The Biratnagar Kings made their NPL debut in the 2024 season. While they had a competitive first season, they showed promising performances and have strategically rebuilt for a stronger showing in 2026.

Who is the captain for the 2026 season?

Sandeep Lamichhane will continue as captain for the 2026 season. His leadership and bowling expertise are crucial to the team's strategy.

Which international players are in the squad?

The Kings have signed several international stars including Faf du Plessis (South Africa), Martin Guptill (New Zealand), George Munsey (Scotland), Marchant de Lange (South Africa), Sam Heazlett (Australia), and Shubham Ranjane (USA).

How can fans support the team?

Fans can support the Biratnagar Kings by attending matches, purchasing official merchandise, engaging with the team on social media, and participating in fan events organized throughout the season.
