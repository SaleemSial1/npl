Chitwan Rhinos

NPL 2026 Team Profile, Squad, and Match Schedule.

The Chitwan Rhinos are one of the most popular teams in the Nepal Premier League. They are known for their exciting playing style and strong community connection. The team keeps improving each season, bringing excitement to fans across Nepal.

The team represents the Chitwan district in central Nepal. They joined the first season of the Nepal Premier League in 2022. Their home ground is Gautam Buddha International Cricket Stadium, but many matches are played at Tribhuvan University Ground in Kathmandu.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the team's history in NPL?

The Chitwan Rhinos made their NPL debut in the 2022 season. They initially struggled but improved quickly, reaching the playoffs in 2023 and the finals in 2024. They have strategically rebuilt for a stronger showing in 2026.

Who is the captain for the 2026 season?

Kushal Malla will lead the team as captain for the 2026 season. His aggressive batting and leadership qualities are crucial to the team's strategy.

Are overseas players confirmed for Season 3?

This page currently shows retained players and auction-bought players from the domestic Season 3 process. Overseas additions will be added only after confirmation.

How can fans support the team?

Fans can support the Chitwan Rhinos by attending matches, purchasing official merchandise, engaging with the team on social media, and participating in fan events organized throughout the season.

Quick Facts

  • Founded: 2022
  • Owner: Nepal T. Twenty Cricket Pvt. Ltd.
  • Franchise Fee: NPR 3.57 Crore
  • Home City: Chitwan, Bagmati Province
  • Home Ground: Gautam Buddha International Cricket Stadium
  • Team Colors: Green & Black
