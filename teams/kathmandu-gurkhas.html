Kathmandu Gurkhas

NPL 2026 Team Profile, Squad, and Match Schedule.

The Kathmandu Gurkhas are an exciting team in the They represent the spirit of Nepali cricket with strong performances and growing popularity. The team mixes local players with international stars and is inspired by the brave Gurkha warriors.

The Kathmandu Gurkhas were created when the Nepal Premier League began in 2024. The team was formed with the goal of developing young Nepali players and bringing in overseas talent. Their identity is based on the courage and strength of Gurkha heritage.

Coaching Staff

The Kathmandu Gurkhas are led by an experienced coaching team focused on developing both individual skills and team cohesion.

Head Coach: Monty Desai

Monty Desai brings extensive T20 coaching experience and a focus on data-driven strategies. His innovative coaching methods and understanding of the modern game make him an ideal leader for the Gurkhas.

Assistant Coach: Binod Das

Former Nepali cricketer Binod Das works closely with local players, focusing on skill development and helping them adapt to the demands of professional T20 cricket. His understanding of Nepali cricket culture is invaluable to the team.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the team's history in NPL?

The Kathmandu Gurkhas made their NPL debut in the 2024 season. They had a mixed performance in their inaugural season but showed promising signs and have strategically rebuilt for a stronger showing in 2026.

Who is the captain for the 2026 season?

Karan KC will lead the team as captain for the 2026 season. His leadership and all-round capabilities are crucial to the team's strategy.

Are overseas players confirmed for Season 3?

This page currently shows retained players and auction-bought players from the domestic Season 3 process. Overseas additions will be added only after confirmation.

How can fans support the team?

Fans can support the Kathmandu Gurkhas by attending matches, purchasing official merchandise, engaging with the team on social media, and participating in fan events organized throughout the season.
