History of Janakpur Bolts

The Janakpur Bolts were created when the Nepal Premier League started in 2024. The league has eight teams from different parts of Nepal. The Bolts were formed to highlight talent from Madhesh Province, bringing together local and foreign players. From the beginning, the team focused on building a strong and balanced lineup, which helped them succeed quickly. They continue to support young players while keeping experienced ones in the squad.

Important Moments

  • Founded in 2024 as one of the main NPL teams.
  • Won the first NPL title by beating Sudurpaschim Royals in a close final.
  • Known for their fast and bold playing style, shown by their lightning bolt logo.

The 2024 Season Performance

In their first season, the Janakpur Bolts performed consistently well. They finished at the top of the league table with strong batting and disciplined bowling. In the final, they chased 185 runs in just 19.2 overs to become champions. This win made them famous across Nepal and showed how strong their team really is.

2024 Highlights

  • High win rate in league matches.
  • Big batting performances and important wickets during key moments.
  • Huge support from fans in Madhesh Province and across the country.

Coaching Staff

The team is coached by former West Indies legend Shivnarine Chanderpaul, who joined before the 2026 season. Along with assistant coaches and fitness trainers, he helps players improve their skills and perform well in important matches. The team also works with educational partners to support young cricketers.

Recent Team Updates

The Bolts have launched a new jersey inspired by Madhesh culture. They are also training hard under Coach Chanderpaul. New signings like Lahiru Samarakoon and Sanjay Krishnamurthi add strength to the lineup. Jimmy Neesham has moved to Pokhara Avengers, increasing the rivalry. The team is active on social media to keep fans updated.

Fan Support and Legacy

The Janakpur Bolts have a strong and loyal fanbase. Their community programs help promote cricket among young students. Their 2024 win inspired many future cricket players. As they move into the 2026 season, the Bolts aim to continue building pride and unity in Nepali cricket.
