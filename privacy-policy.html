1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

  • Information You Give Us: When you contact us by email or through forms, we may collect your name, email, phone number, or any other information you provide.
  • Account Details: Right now, our site doesn't require user accounts. If we add features like ticket sales or fan zones later, we may collect usernames, passwords, and preferences.

b. Non-Personal Information

  • Automatic Data: We may collect data such as your IP address, browser type, device info, operating system, pages viewed, and visit time. This helps us understand how people use our site.
  • Cookies: We may use cookies to improve your experience. Cookies are small files stored on your device. You can manage or delete cookies in your browser settings. See our Cookie Policy for more info.

c. Third-Party Data

If you use third-party content (like YouTube videos or social media links), those services may collect information based on their own privacy policies.

We do not collect personal information from children under 13. If we find out we have such data, we will delete it.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To run and maintain the website and share NPL updates, news, and match information.
  • To reply to your questions or feedback.
  • To improve website features and performance.
  • To send newsletters or updates if you choose to receive them.
  • To meet legal requirements or protect our website and users.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. Sharing Your Information

  • Service Providers: We may share data with trusted partners who help us run the site (e.g., web hosting or analytics).
  • Legal Reasons: We may share data if required by law or court order.
  • Business Changes: If NPL merges or is sold, your data may be transferred as part of the process.
  • Your Consent: We only share data for other reasons if you give permission.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our site may use cookies to remember preferences, improve performance, and analyze traffic. Some third-party tools like Google Analytics may also use cookies. You can opt out of Google Analytics by visiting this link.

You can disable cookies in your browser, but some parts of the site may not work properly.

5. Data Security

We use security measures like encryption and secure servers to protect your data. However, no online system is 100% safe, and we cannot guarantee complete security.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your country, you may have rights to:

  • See, correct, or delete your information.
  • Object to or limit how we use your data.
  • Withdraw consent for certain uses.
  • Ask for a copy of your data.

To use these rights, contact us at the email below. You can also unsubscribe from our emails anytime.

7. International Data Transfers

Our website may be hosted in Nepal or another country. If you visit from outside Nepal, your data may be stored in other countries, but we follow the law to keep it safe.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be shown here with a new effective date. Please review it regularly.
