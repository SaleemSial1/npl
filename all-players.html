NPL 2026 Players Overview

The 2026 NPL will run from November 17 to December 13, 2026. It includes eight teams playing a total of 32 matches. The format follows a double round-robin and playoff system, giving teams many chances to perform well. International players will also take part, making the competition more exciting for fans.

The knockout matches will be held as follows:

  • Qualifier 1 – December 9
  • Eliminator – December 10
  • Qualifier 2 – December 11
  • Final – December 13

Stadiums across Nepal are being prepared to host large crowds and provide fans with an amazing cricket experience.

The NPL 2026 Auction Process

The NPL auction took place on August 9, 2026, in Kathmandu. Out of 458 registered players, 150 were shortlisted for bidding. Each team was allowed to keep up to seven players from last season. The auction saw some high prices for top players — some were bought for as much as 15 lakh Nepali rupees. This event shaped the teams and increased excitement for the new season.
