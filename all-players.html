The Nepal Premier League (NPL) is one of the most followed cricket events in Nepal. The 2026 season brings retained players, auction picks, and overseas names together for Season 3. This guide covers the player pool, team squads, auction context, and upcoming matches in one place.

NPL 2026 Players Overview

The 2026 NPL is expected during the November-December 2026 window. It includes eight teams playing a total of 32 matches. The format follows a league stage and playoff system, giving every team seven group matches before the top four move forward. International players will also take part, making the competition more exciting for fans.

The knockout matches will be held as follows:

  • Qualifier 1 – December 9
  • Eliminator – December 10
  • Qualifier 2 – December 11
  • Final – the confirmed final date

The Season 3 schedule lists matches at Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, giving teams a consistent venue across the tournament.
