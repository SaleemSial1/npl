Team Standings, Rankings & Statistics

The Nepal Premier League (NPL Season 3) promises competitive cricket with eight teams fighting for the championship. Fans are following the tournament as it builds on the previous editions. With the schedule listed and teams preparing, the stage is set for a full season of franchise T20 cricket.

The Nepal Premier League is a major T20 cricket tournament organized by the Cricket Association of Nepal. This third season will feature franchise teams from different cities and provinces. Sponsored by Siddhartha Bank, the event is expected during the November-December 2026 window, with matches listed at Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur.

The league uses a round-robin format, where each team plays against every other team once. The top four teams will move on to the playoffs — Qualifiers and Eliminator — leading up to the final. This setup gives every team a fair chance to prove their strength throughout the tournament.

Tournament Format and Schedule

Each of the eight teams will play seven league matches, making a total of 28 games in the group stage. After that:

  • Qualifier 1: December 9 (Top 2 teams)
  • Eliminator: December 10 (3rd vs 4th place)
  • Qualifier 2: December 11
  • Final: the confirmed final date

Matches are expected to include both day and night windows once the final fixture list is released. The confirmed opening game, doubleheader days and playoff timing should be checked from the official Season 3 schedule before readers treat any date as final.

How the Points System Works in NPL 2026

  • Win = 2 points
  • Tie or No Result = 1 point
  • Loss = 0 points

If two or more teams have the same number of points, the Net Run Rate (NRR) decides their position in the standings. Teams must play smart — scoring quickly and bowling tightly — to keep their NRR high and stay competitive.

Team Previews and Key Players

Top Contenders

  • Janakpur Bolts: Defending champions led by Aasif Sheikh and coached by Shivnarine Chanderpaul. Known for their balance and experience.
  • Biratnagar Kings: Captained by Sandeep Lamichhane and coached by Pubudu Dassanayake. Strong in spin and strategy.
  • Chitwan Rhinos: Led by Kushal Malla, focusing on youth and power hitting.
  • Karnali Yaks: Led by Sompal Kami, coached by Gyanendra Malla, with a focus on pace bowling.
  • Kathmandu Gorkhas: Captain Karan KC and coach Monty Desai bring aggression and tactics.
  • Lumbini Lions: Led by Rohit Paudel with coach Tinu Yohannan, focusing on discipline and team strategy.
  • Pokhara Avengers: Captain Kushal Bhurtel leads a young and ambitious team.
  • Sudurpaschim Royals: Led by Dipendra Singh Airee, looking for redemption after last season's final loss.
