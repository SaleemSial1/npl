The Nepal Premier League (NPL) is becoming one of the most watched cricket tournaments in Nepal. In 2026, overseas players will join local Nepali talent to make Season 3 more competitive. NPL 2026 International Stars

The Nepal Premier League (NPL) is a T20 cricket league organized by the Cricket Association of Nepal. It aims to promote local players and bring global attention to Nepali cricket. The 2026 season builds on last year's success with better organization, stronger teams, and more excitement. Eight teams will compete in a round-robin format before heading to the playoffs and finals. The league also supports young talent by requiring teams to include players under 21 years old.
