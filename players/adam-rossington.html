Adam Rossington Player Profile

Early Life and Career Beginnings

Adam Rossington was born on May 5, 1993, in Edgware, Middlesex, England. He studied at Mill Hill School, where he developed his passion for cricket. From a young age, he demonstrated natural talent as a right-handed batsman and wicketkeeper. He progressed through the Middlesex youth system, making rapid improvements. In 2009, he scored 161 in his second XI debut against Northamptonshire, an impressive innings that caught selectors' attention and paved the way for his professional debut.

Domestic Career Highlights

Rossington spent eight productive years with Northamptonshire, where he built a strong reputation. He played a crucial role in the team's T20 Blast victory and later became captain. His leadership was instrumental in the team's promotion to Division One in 2019. In 2022, he moved to Essex on loan before earning a permanent contract until 2026. He took over as wicketkeeper and performed strongly across formats before focusing exclusively on T20 cricket.

International League Experiences

Rossington has showcased his talents in numerous global T20 leagues. He represented London Spirit in the Hundred, where his explosive batting stood out. He also played for Dubai Capitals in the ILT20 and Sunrisers Eastern Cape in the SA20. In the USA's National Cricket League, he featured for Los Angeles Waves and New York Lions. More recently, he joined Lahore Qalandars for the Global Super League in 2026. Adapting to diverse conditions has been crucial to his development as a versatile T20 specialist.

Adam Rossington in Nepal Premier League
