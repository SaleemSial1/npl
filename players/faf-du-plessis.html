Faf du Plessis Player Profile

Early Life and Cricket Journey

Faf du Plessis was born on July 13, 1984, in Pretoria, South Africa. He grew up playing multiple sports but ultimately chose cricket as his main focus. He joined the Titans in the early 2000s, where his talent quickly became apparent.

Domestic Career

He became a consistent batsman in South African domestic cricket, representing teams like the Titans and Lancashire. His strong performances in T20 cricket and experience in England helped refine his skills and prepare him for international success.

International Career

Faf made his international debut in 2011 and soon became an integral player across all formats. His leadership qualities earned him the captaincy of the national team, where he led South Africa with distinction.

Franchise Cricket Career

Faf has been a standout performer in T20 leagues worldwide, scoring over 11,000 T20 runs. He was a key player for Chennai Super Kings and Royal Challengers Bangalore in the IPL. In 2026, he plays for Delhi Capitals. His global experience includes stints in CPL, BBL, SA20, and Major League Cricket.

Faf du Plessis in NPL 2026

NPL 2026 will be held from November 17 to December 13 with 32 exciting matches. Faf's inclusion adds significant star power to the tournament.

Joining Biratnagar Kings

Faf signed with Biratnagar Kings, joining a team led by Sandeep Lamichhane and featuring George Munsey. His arrival significantly increases the league's popularity and brings international experience to the squad.

Expected Role

He will likely play as an opener or top-order batsman, providing stability to the innings. His experience will be invaluable in guiding younger players, and he adds further value with his exceptional fielding and occasional leg-spin bowling.

Leadership & Achievements

  • Captained South Africa in all three formats (2017-2021)
  • Won multiple IPL titles with Chennai Super Kings
  • First South African to score centuries in all three formats
  • Named South African Cricketer of the Year in 2019
  • Led South Africa to Test series victories in Australia and New Zealand

Personal Life

Faf is married to Imari Visser and has two children. Outside of cricket, he maintains a strong fitness regimen, enjoys golf, and is actively involved in charity work. He's particularly passionate about wildlife conservation causes in South Africa.
