Rohit Paudel Player Profile

Early Life and Inspiration

Rohit Paudel was born on September 2, 2002, in Nawalparasi, Nepal. He became interested in cricket after watching Nepal play in the 2014 ICC World T20. With strong family support, he trained daily and improved his skills even with limited resources.

Career Beginnings and Debuts

List A Debut: February 8, 2018
ODI Debut: August 3, 2018 vs Netherlands
T20I Debut: January 31, 2019 vs UAE

He was Nepal's top scorer in the 2018 ACC U-19 Asia Cup with 161 runs.

Key Youth Achievements

Rohit quickly established himself as a promising talent in youth cricket with several notable performances that caught the attention of selectors and cricket enthusiasts.

Rise to Captaincy

Rohit became Nepal's captain in November 2022 at just 20 years old. Under his leadership, Nepal won the 2023 ACC Men's Premier Cup and qualified for the 2023 Asia Cup. His outstanding performances earned him the NSJF Player of the Year award in 2024.
