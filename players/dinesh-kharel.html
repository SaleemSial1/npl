Dinesh Kharel

NPL 2026 Profile, Stats, and Career Insights

Pokhara Avengers

Dinesh Kharel is a rising star in Nepal cricket. He is known for his left-handed batting and slow left-arm spin bowling. Born in the beautiful district of Gulmi, he represents Nepal's growing strength in world cricket. His story of hard work and dedication inspires many young athletes across the country.

Dinesh Kharel Player Profile

Early Life and Background

Dinesh Kharel was born on April 9, 2002, in Gulmi, Nepal. He grew up in a simple family and loved cricket from a young age. He practiced on local grounds and quickly stood out because of his natural talent. His local coaches saw his potential and helped him train seriously. With strong support from his family, he balanced his studies and cricket practice, preparing for a professional career in the sport.

Entry into Professional Cricket

Dinesh started his journey in youth cricket by playing for Nepal's Under-19 team around 2016. His good performances helped him join different domestic teams. Scouts noticed his all-round skills, and he began playing in national tournaments. With help from coaches and mentors, he improved his batting and bowling techniques and gained valuable experience.

Dinesh Kharel's Role in the Nepal National Team

Dinesh plays as a batting all-rounder for the Nepal national cricket team. He provides important runs in the middle order and also bowls left-arm spin when needed. His role helps maintain balance in the team during limited-overs matches. Playing alongside senior players has improved his understanding of the game and made him an important part of the team's future plans.

Performance in Domestic Leagues

Domestic cricket has been a key stage in Dinesh's career. He has played for several regional teams and performed consistently. Playing for the Pokhara Avengers gave him great exposure to top-level competition. His reliable performances under pressure helped him gain recognition and prepare for international challenges.

Dinesh Kharel Nepal Cricketer NPL 2026 Profile
