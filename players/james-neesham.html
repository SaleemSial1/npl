James Neesham

NPL 2026 Profile, Stats, and Career Insights

Pokhara Avengers

James Neesham brings New Zealand's dynamic all-rounder to the Nepal Premier League. Known for his explosive batting, clever medium-pace bowling, and exceptional fielding, Neesham adds international experience and match-winning capabilities to the Pokhara Avengers lineup. His journey from domestic cricket to global T20 leagues showcases his versatility and ability to perform under pressure.

James Neesham Player Profile

Early Life and Career Beginnings

James Neesham was born on September 17, 1990, in Auckland, New Zealand. Growing up in a sports-loving family, he developed his passion for cricket from childhood. He honed his skills through school and club cricket before making his domestic debut for Otago in the 2010–11 season. His breakthrough performances impressed selectors, though injuries occasionally interrupted his progress. Despite setbacks, he consistently returned stronger, showcasing his all-round talent in List A and first-class matches.

Domestic Achievements

In New Zealand's domestic cricket, Neesham has been a standout performer for Otago and Wellington. His explosive batting includes a best T20 score of 97 not out, while his bowling highlights feature impressive figures of 5-44 in domestic cricket. In first-class cricket, he has scored over 3,300 runs and claimed more than 130 wickets. His aggressive approach and consistent performances have made him a sought-after player in franchise cricket worldwide.

International Cricket Journey

Neesham made his international debut in 2012 in a T20I against South Africa and has since represented New Zealand across all formats. He has played crucial roles in major tournaments including World Cups and Champions Trophy events. While he has faced form fluctuations and injuries, his resilience has kept him relevant in international cricket. In recent years, he has focused primarily on white-ball formats where his all-round skills are most valuable.

Performance in T20 Leagues

Neesham has excelled in numerous T20 leagues worldwide. He debuted in the IPL in 2014 with Delhi Daredevils and later represented Kolkata Knight Riders and Mumbai Indians. His performances in other leagues like the BPL and ILT20 have been particularly impressive. In the BPL, he scored 138 runs in just four games, including two fifties. His adaptability and all-round capabilities make him a valuable asset in franchise cricket, with recent stints including the SA20 with Durban Super Giants.

James Neesham in Nepal Premier League

The Nepal Premier League represents an exciting chapter in Neesham's career. In the 2024 season, he was instrumental in Janakpur Bolts' title victory, scoring 196 runs in nine innings with an average of 35.28. His power-hitting was evident as he hit the most sixes (23) in the tournament. For the 2026 season, he has moved to Pokhara Avengers, bringing his experience and match-winning abilities to the team.

Role with Pokhara Avengers

For James Neesham NPL 2026, he joins Pokhara Avengers as a premier all-rounder. He is expected to bat in the middle order, providing both stability and explosive finishing, while also bowling crucial overs with his medium pace. His international experience will be invaluable in guiding younger Nepali players.
