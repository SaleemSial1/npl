Kushal Bhurtel Player Profile

Early Life and Background

Kushal Bhurtel was born on January 22, 1997, in Butwal, Rupendehi. He started playing cricket at a young age and showed great skill early on. His family supported his dream and helped him train hard. He learned from international players and soon got noticed by selectors. Over time, his talent and hard work helped him become one of Nepal's top players.

International Debut and Career Milestones

Bhurtel made his T20I debut against the Netherlands on April 17, 2021. Later, he played his first ODI match against Papua New Guinea. He quickly gained fame for his consistent performances. During one of his early tournaments, he scored 278 runs and was named Player of the Series. He has now played more than 120 matches, scoring nearly 3,000 runs, including a century in limited-overs cricket.

Batting Achievements

Bhurtel is a right-handed batsman known for his attacking style. His highest ODI score is 115, and his strike rate in T20 matches is often above 120. He can play in different positions depending on the team's needs, making him a flexible and reliable player.

Performance in Domestic Leagues

Before playing internationally, Bhurtel made a name for himself in domestic cricket. In the 2026 Prime Minister Cup, he scored 88 runs in one of the matches. He has also performed well in regional and franchise tournaments, improving his skills against tough opponents. His fitness and focus make him a key player in domestic competitions.

Kushal Bhurtel in NPL 2026

In the Nepal Premier League 2026, Bhurtel is a key player and team captain. He leads with confidence and focuses on team growth. His batting in powerplays has been aggressive, and his leg-spin bowling helps in tight moments. As of November 2026, he has made valuable contributions with both bat and ball, earning respect from fans and teammates.

Bowling Contributions

Besides batting, Bhurtel is a useful leg-spin bowler. He has taken over 30 wickets in T20Is with an economy rate below 7. His variations and control make him a threat to batters. In 2026, he recorded 4 wickets for 19 runs in a World Cup match, showing his all-round ability.

Recent Achievements in 2026

This year, Bhurtel has been in top form. He scored 69 runs against Oman and 92 runs against UAE, both match-winning performances. He has also led his NPL team with smart strategies, earning praise for his leadership. In his last 10 T20Is, he has scored over 387 runs at an average above 55.

Playing Style and Strengths

Bhurtel's game combines power and smart shot placement. He plays strong drives and pulls against fast bowlers and moves well against spin.

Batting Technique

Plays strong drives and pulls against fast bowlers with excellent timing and placement. Known for his attacking style and ability to score quickly.

Leg-Spin Bowling

Uses smart variations and control in his leg-spin bowling to contain batsmen. His googly is particularly effective.

Leadership Skills

As team captain, demonstrates strong leadership with smart strategies and the ability to inspire teammates.

His fitness and focus make him one of Nepal's most complete players in modern cricket.

Future Prospects

Kushal Bhurtel has established himself as a key player in Nepal cricket. With consistent NPL performances and strong international showings, he continues to be a vital asset to the national team. Opportunities in foreign leagues might also come his way. If he keeps working on his fitness and skills, he could become one of Nepal's most celebrated players in the coming years.
