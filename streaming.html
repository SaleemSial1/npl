The (NPL) 2026 is full of action and excitement for cricket fans. This second season features eight teams and many famous players from around the world. The tournament runs from November 17 to December 13, 2026. Fans can watch all matches live through official TV channels and streaming apps.

Introduction to Nepal Premier League 2026

The Nepal Premier League returns stronger in 2026, showing Nepal's growing cricket talent to the world. There will be 32 matches in a round-robin format followed by playoffs. Eight teams will compete for the title, and players like Faf du Plessis will join the league. All matches will take place at one main venue in Kirtipur.
