What is Nepal Premier League 2026?

Nepal Premier League 2026 is the third season of Nepal's franchise T20 cricket competition, bringing eight teams together for a tournament window from November 17 to December 13. NPL Season 3 Official Guide

Nepal Premier League 2026 Tournament Format

🏆

T20 Round-Robin Format

8 franchises battle in a single round-robin league stage (28 matches), with the top 4 teams advancing to the playoffs.

⚡

Playoff System

IPL-style playoffs: Qualifier 1 (1st vs 2nd), Eliminator (3rd vs 4th), Qualifier 2, followed by the Grand Final in Kathmandu.

💡

Floodlit TU Ground

Matches hosted at the upgraded Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground, Kirtipur featuring high-grade floodlights.

🌍

Overseas & Domestic Core

Each squad features marquee domestic captains, retained stars, and up to 4 international marquee signings.

When will Nepal Premier League 2026 start?

Nepal Premier League 2026 is listed for a November 17 start, with the tournament window running through December 13.

How many teams play in NPL 2026?

Eight franchises are part of NPL 2026: Janakpur Bolts, Kathmandu Gurkhas, Chitwan Rhinos, Karnali Yaks, Biratnagar Kings, Pokhara Avengers, Lumbini Lions and Sudurpaschim Royals.
