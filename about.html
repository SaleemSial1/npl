About NPL

Nepal Premier League - Season 3

Established: 2024

The Nepal Premier League (NPL) is the top T20 cricket tournament in Nepal. It is organized by the Cricket Association of Nepal (CAN). Started in 2024, the NPL brings together the best Nepali and international players in fast, exciting matches. It is the biggest cricket event in Nepal and helps promote the game, develop young players, and give new talent a chance to shine on a big stage.

History and Vision

The NPL was created to make cricket more professional in Nepal and to build a strong cricket system for players and fans. The first season in 2024 was a big success, with huge crowds and great viewership. The 2026 season will be even bigger, with more coverage and tough competition.

Our goal is to make NPL a world-class league that inspires young cricketers and brings people together through sports and teamwork across Nepal.

Tournament Format

  • Format: T20 cricket — short, exciting matches full of action.
  • Structure: Teams play in a round-robin stage, then the top teams move to the playoffs and final.
  • Season Details (2026): 8 teams will play 29 matches across 3 venues in Nepal from November 17 to December 15, 2026.
  • Player Auction: Players are chosen through an auction system with different categories. In 2026, 150 players took part, forming well-balanced teams.

Participating Teams

The NPL has 8 teams, each representing a major region of Nepal:

Chitwan Region

Each team is owned by well-known business groups and includes local, international, and young players.

Broadcast and Reach

NPL matches are broadcast live in 15 countries across Asia and Europe. Fans can watch live games and get updates through our official website, social media, and TV partners.

Commitment to Cricket Development

The NPL also supports the growth of cricket in Nepal. We work with CAN to run youth programs, training camps, and community projects. We also help build cricket grounds and scout new talent to ensure a strong future for the sport.
