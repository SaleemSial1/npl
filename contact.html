Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about the Nepal Premier League.

How can I purchase tickets for NPL matches?
Please use official ticketing partners and team or league announcements before buying tickets. Our ticket pages are informational guides and do not sell official match tickets directly.
How can my company become a sponsor of NPL?
We welcome website partnership, media, advertising, and guest-post inquiries. Please contact partnerships@nplcricketleague.com with your company details and campaign goals.
How can media organizations get accreditation?
We do not issue official match accreditation. Media organizations can contact us at info@nplcricketleague.com for website coverage, corrections, interviews, or content partnerships.
Are there volunteering opportunities with NPL?
We do not manage tournament volunteer positions. For website writing, research, or content collaboration, email admin@nplcricketleague.com with your background and area of interest.
How can I report technical issues with the website?
For technical issues with this website, please contact admin@nplcricketleague.com. Include the page URL, your device, browser, and any error message you saw.
