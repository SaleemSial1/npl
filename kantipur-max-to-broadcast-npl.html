Kantipur Max HD Will Broadcast NPL 2026

Cricket fans in Nepal are excited because the (NPL) 2026 is back. Kantipur Max HD will show all matches live for free in HD quality. The deal was confirmed on November 16, 2026.

Star Sports will broadcast NPL matches internationally, while DishHome Go will stream them online in Nepal. This means more people worldwide can enjoy NPL 2026.

Kantipur Max HD as Broadcaster of NPL 2026

The NPL started in 2024 and is now Nepal's biggest T20 league. The 2026 season runs from November 17 to December 13. Eight teams will play league matches followed by playoffs.

Top Nepali players like Sandeep Lamichhane, Rohit Paudel, and Dipendra Singh Airee will play this season. Most matches will be held at Tribhuvan University Cricket Ground.
